/// TRADINGVIEW STARTER GUIDE

READ THE CHART

CANDLESTICK
A bar showing price movement for one period.

O / H / L / C
Open, highest, lowest and closing price for that candle.

TIMEFRAME
1m, 30m, 1h and 1D show one minute, 30 minutes, one hour or one day per candle.

VOLUME
How much was traded. Bigger bars often mean more activity.

RSI
A momentum guide. High can mean overbought; low can mean oversold.

MACD
Compares momentum lines to help spot trend changes.

MOVING AVERAGE (MA)
A smoothed average price that helps show the trend.

INDICATORS
Extra chart tools such as RSI, MACD and MA. Use them as clues, not answers.

PRICE AXIS
The right-side scale shows the price level.

CHART / DRAWING TOOLS
Use the toolbar to change view, add lines, or mark areas on the chart.

BUY / BULLISH
Bullish means price may rise. It is not a guarantee to buy.

SELL / BEARISH
Bearish means price may fall. It is not a guarantee to sell.

SUPPORT
A price area where a fall has previously slowed or bounced.

RESISTANCE
A price area where a rise has previously slowed or turned down.

EDUCATION ONLY — TradeLeague is a simulation. Chart terms and signals are not financial advice.
