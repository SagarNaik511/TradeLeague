READ THE TRADINGVIEW CHART

01 // CHART BASICS

CANDLESTICK
One candle shows price movement during the chosen timeframe. Its body spans open to close; the wicks show the high and low. A close above open is bullish; a close below open is bearish.

OPEN (O)
The first price of that candle's timeframe.

HIGH (H)
The highest price reached during that timeframe.

LOW (L)
The lowest price reached during that timeframe.

CLOSE (C)
The final price of that timeframe; it helps show whether the candle finished up or down.

TIMEFRAME
1m, 30m, 1h and 1D mean one minute, 30 minutes, one hour and one day per candle. Shorter views show more detail; longer views show the broader trend.

VOLUME
How much was traded during a period. Higher volume can show stronger interest behind a move; low volume can mean less conviction.

PRICE AXIS
The scale on the right shows price levels. Read it with the time axis below the chart.

CHART / DRAWING TOOLS
Use the TradingView toolbar to change chart type, add trend lines, or mark areas. They help you observe; they do not predict prices.

02 // INDICATORS

RSI
A 0–100 momentum measure. Below 30 is commonly called oversold and above 70 overbought, but neither level guarantees a rebound or fall.

MACD
Compares two moving averages to show momentum. When the MACD line crosses the signal line it can suggest momentum is changing, not a guaranteed direction.

MOVING AVERAGE (MA)
Smooths past prices to show the general trend. Price above or below an MA gives context, not an automatic buy or sell signal.

INDICATORS
Extra tools such as RSI, MACD and MA. Compare them with price and volume instead of relying on one reading.

03 // TRADING BASICS

BUY / BULLISH
Bullish means you expect price to rise. It is an outlook, not a promise that buying will profit.

SELL / BEARISH
Bearish means you expect price to fall. It is an outlook, not a guarantee that selling is right.

SUPPORT
An area where falling price has often slowed or bounced. It is usually a zone, not an exact guaranteed price.

RESISTANCE
An area where rising price has often slowed or turned down. It is usually a zone, not an exact guaranteed price.
